Beyond the Powerhouse.
Mitochondria have been know for decades to play an essential role in ATP synthesis, however they are much  more complex than just ATP producing machines.
Mitochondria can control cell death and survival.
If a cell has become damaged or under stress , the mitochondria will release certain proteins (such as cytochrome c) into the cytosol .
This causes a controlled chain reaction which destroys the cell safely.
This stops the body from becoming cancerous and diseased.
Mitochondria are constantly undergoing fission and fusion and are transported to high demand areas.
They regulate calcium signalling which is essential for muscle contraction and nerve transmissions.
Mitochondria also create essential molecules such as lipids and iron-sulphur clusters.
Not all of the energy created by mitochondria becomes ATP .
In brown adipose tissue (brown fat) there are special proteins which cause mitochondria to become uncoupled and therefore release energy as heat .
This is known as non shivering thermogenesis and is essential for body temperature regulation.
Mitochondria also play an essential role as the first line of defence against pathogens .
If mitochondria become infected by viruses or bacteria they will release mitochondrial DNA and reactive oxygen species to trigger an innate immune response by alerting the cell.
Overall mitochondria are highly versatile and intelligent master regulators of metabolism , stress responses and overall cell fate .
